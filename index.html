Honest about what an app can't do

Studies show that photo apps often misidentify poisonous mushrooms. Wildlens never tells you a wild mushroom or plant is safe to eat, and always points you to experts and emergency services.

Wildlens is an informational field guide. It does not replace experts, doctors or rescue services. In an emergency call 112 (Europe) or 911 (North America). In Italy, also install GeoResQ by the Soccorso Alpino.
PREPARED AND INSURED

Go to the mountains prepared and insured

In 2025 Italian mountain rescue carried out more than 13,000 missions, a record. In many Alpine regions an uninjured person rescued by helicopter can pay up to 750–800 €. Wildlens prepares you for free, and soon lets you activate rescue and accident cover for a day or a year in the app.

Questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The guide, the Ask Taylor answers, the map pins, GPS, tracking and SOS tools work offline. Download Taylor's brain and your area packs before you leave, with Wi-Fi.

Which phones can recognize photos offline?

The light brain (about 150 MB) runs on almost any phone. The full brain (Gemma 4, about 2 GB) needs a recent phone with Chrome and WebGPU.

Is it free?

The app is free to try. A Premium plan with every area pack is planned.

Do I need an account?

No. Your journal, photos and route stay on your phone.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes: open it in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen". Native Android and iOS apps are on the way.
