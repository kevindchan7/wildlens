EMERGENCY

SOS

112 · 911

112 works across Europe, 911 in the US and Canada. Both often work with no credit and on another carrier's network. In the mountains ask for mountain rescue.

  1. Where you are: coordinates (Map tab), trail, altitude.
  2. What happened and how many people.
  3. Injured: conscious, breathing, bleeding.
  4. Keep your phone on and the line free.

Get found

Alpine distress signal: 6 signals a minute, a minute's pause, repeat. Reply: 3 a minute.

  • Helicopter: both arms up in a Y = "yes, we need help". One arm up, one down = "no".
  • Three fires, three whistles or three of anything mean distress.
